点一段正文 · 其余自己淡去

Writing well is not a matter of the right app, the perfect font or a certain tool. It requires focus—good writing requires your full, undivided attention.

If you want to write well—in fact, if you want to do anything well—you need to concentrate. There are many ways to succeed at writing, and there are even more ways to fail.

However, successful writing is never a matter of luck. To write well, you need to be fully awake, dead serious and unconditionally enjoy what you do—like a child, when it plays.

iA Writer 首页自己在演示 Focus：未聚焦的段落褪成浅灰，当前段保持 #1a1a1a，一条青蓝光标停在句末。点一下换段，邻居自己让位。

Polarsteps · 打开实时位置的绿灯开关

浅色列表 · 绿灯开关 · 地图预览卡

  2. ~3sLocation privacy：开关关闭，预览卡停在 OFF
